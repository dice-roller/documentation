@dice-roller/rpg-dice-roller / presenters / TreePresenter

Abstract Class: TreePresenter<T>

Defined in: src/presenters/TreePresenter.ts:28

A presenter with a hook per result type.

present() dispatches a history to history(), a roll to roll(), and a result node to its hook (via visit()). Subclasses implement the hooks and control traversal: call visit() for child results and die() from pool() for individual dice as needed. die() is not dispatched by present() or visit(); an individual die is not an ExpressionResult.
