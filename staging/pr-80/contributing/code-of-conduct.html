Code of Conduct ​

Our pledge ​

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

Our standards ​

Help make the community welcoming by:

  • Treating others with empathy and kindness.
  • Respecting different opinions, experiences and levels of knowledge.
  • Giving constructive feedback and receiving it thoughtfully.
  • Discussing ideas and code without attacking the people behind them.
  • Taking responsibility for mistakes, apologising where appropriate and learning from them.
  • Respecting other people's time and boundaries. Contributions and support are provided voluntarily, and responses may take time.

Unacceptable behaviour includes:

  • Harassment, intimidation, threats or discriminatory abuse.
  • Trolling, insults, derogatory comments and personal attacks.
  • Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
  • Publishing or sharing someone's private information without their explicit permission.
  • Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern or participates in an investigation.
  • Other behaviour that reasonably makes participation unsafe or unwelcoming.

Disagreement and constructive criticism are welcome. They should remain respectful and focused on the subject being discussed.

Scope ​

This Code of Conduct applies to all project community spaces, including issues, pull requests, Discussions and other project communication channels. It also covers project-related harassment through private messages or external channels.

It applies when someone officially represents the project in public, such as through an official account or as an appointed representative at an event.

Reporting a concern ​

Report concerns privately to the project maintainers at conduct@greenimp.co.uk. Please do not open a public issue to report an incident.

You can report behaviour you experienced or witnessed. Include any relevant links, context or screenshots you feel comfortable sharing, where available. You do not need to provide a complete account or evidence before raising a concern.

Reports will be reviewed fairly, and maintainers will respond as soon as reasonably possible. The project is maintained voluntarily, so response times may vary.

Reports will be handled discreetly. Information will be shared only where necessary to investigate the concern or take appropriate action, with care for the privacy and safety of those involved.

Enforcement ​

Maintainers are responsible for applying this Code of Conduct fairly. They may remove, edit or reject contributions, restrict interactions, or temporarily or permanently exclude someone from project spaces.

Decisions will take account of the severity and impact of the behaviour, its context and any previous incidents. The responses below are guidelines, not mandatory steps: serious incidents may result in an immediate temporary or permanent ban.

Maintainers will explain moderation decisions to those affected where appropriate, while respecting the privacy of others involved.

Correction ​

For an inappropriate comment or other less serious incident, maintainers may explain the concern privately and ask for the behaviour to stop or the content to be corrected.

Warning ​

For more serious or repeated behaviour, maintainers may issue a warning explaining what must change and the consequences of further violations. This may include a requirement to stop contacting particular people.

Temporary ban ​

Serious or sustained misconduct may result in a temporary ban from project spaces. Maintainers will communicate its duration and any conditions, including restrictions on contacting those involved.

Permanent ban ​

Severe misconduct, repeated violations or attempts to evade restrictions may result in permanent exclusion from the project community.

Retaliation, continued harassment or breaches of these restrictions may lead to further action.

Attribution ​
