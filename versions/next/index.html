Familiar dice notation

Roll d20 + 5, 2d% or 4dF. Combine different dice, group rolls and use maths in the same expression.

Roll by your game’s rules

Keep the highest dice, reroll ones, explode on a maximum roll or count successes. Combine modifiers to express the mechanics your game uses.

Make the results your own

Use the roll total or inspect the individual dice. Build a presenter plugin to format the results for your app, chat bot or virtual tabletop.
